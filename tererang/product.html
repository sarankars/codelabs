A masterclass in asymmetric volume. The Gulabi Drape Dress balances a sculpted bodice with cascading pleats that gather into an effortless side cowl. Crafted for movement, this statement piece transitions effortlessly from sunset celebrations to intimate dinners.

  • Asymmetrical cowl neckline with concealed side closure
  • Sculpted high-low draped hemline that moves with every stride
  • Discreet inseam pockets for everyday practicality

Woven from 100% premium botanical modal-silk. Breathable, gentle against sensitive skin, with a liquid-like lustre that catches the light with refined subtlety.
